📊 Blinkit Analysis Dashboard – Final Project Report
🔹 1. Abstract

The Blinkit Analysis Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project designed to analyze and monitor key business metrics of a quick-commerce platform. This dashboard provides insights into sales performance, order distribution, profit trends, delivery efficiency, and customer behavior across multiple cities. It enables stakeholders to make data-driven decisions by offering a comprehensive view of operational and financial performance.

🔹 2. Objectives
To analyze total sales, orders, and profit across cities and categories
To evaluate delivery partner performance and efficiency
To understand customer purchasing behavior and payment preferences
To identify high-performing products and categories

🔹 6. Dashboard Features & Insights
📍 City-wise Analysis
Mumbai and Coimbatore show the highest number of orders (177 each)
Hyderabad has the lowest order count (152)
🏬 Warehouse Performance
Warehouses WH-A1 to WH-D4 efficiently support order distribution across cities
🚚 Delivery Partner Analysis
Top-performing partners: Mumbai & Coimbatore (177 deliveries)
Slight variation observed across other cities indicating balanced distribution
💰 Profit Analysis
Highest profit city: Delhi (~₹28,493)
Categories like Beverages generate the highest profit (~₹38,396)
🛒 Category Performance
Top Sales Category: Beverages
Consistent performers: Dairy, Fruits & Vegetables, Snacks
Category ratings indicate strong customer satisfaction in Beverages
📉 Discount Analysis
Discount distribution is balanced across categories
Fruits & Vegetables and Household categories receive moderate discounts
💳 Payment Method Insights
Most preferred: Debit Card (269) and Cash on Delivery (261)
Least used: Credit Card (228)
📦 Quantity Analysis
Products like Paneer, Tea, and Juice show high demand
Variability indicates diverse customer preferences
📈 Delivery Trends
Swiggy Genie has the highest average delivery performance
Gradual decrease observed across other partners

🔹 9. Conclusion

The Blinkit Analysis Dashboard successfully provides a 360-degree view of business performance. It highlights key growth areas, customer trends, and operational efficiency. The insights derived from this dashboard can help improve decision-making in inventory management, marketing strategies, and delivery optimization.
